Assess this contract for material risks.

For each risk you identify, return:
  - risk: short label (e.g. "Unlimited liability", "Asymmetric termination")
  - severity: Low | Medium | High | Critical
  - clause_ref: the clause number where the risk is grounded (e.g. "7.2")
  - reasoning: 1-2 sentences explaining the risk, citing the clause text

Severity calibration:
  - Low: minor concern, accept without negotiation
  - Medium: should be reviewed by the requesting team
  - High: significant concern, usually requires negotiation
  - Critical: deal-breaker, escalate to legal immediately

You also have access to similar prior clauses from the company's contract
history, along with how those were rated. Use these as comparators when
calibrating severity. A clause that closely resembles a previously-Critical
finding should usually be Critical too.

After listing findings, provide an aggregate score 0-100:
  - 0-30   → low risk overall
  - 31-60  → moderate risk (manager review)
  - 61-100 → high risk (legal review)

Strict rules:
  - Only report risks grounded in the actual clause text — no hypothetical concerns.
  - Use clause numbers exactly as written (e.g. "7.2", not "Section 7.2").
  - If no material risks exist, return findings: [] and a low score.
  - Do NOT invent comparators that aren't shown below.
